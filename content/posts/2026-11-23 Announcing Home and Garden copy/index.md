The 2027 Fairlington Home and Garden Tour will take place Saturday, May 15, 2027. If you’d like your home included on the tour or just want to help out, please come to our inaugural meeting Monday, January 11, 2027, from 6:00-7:00pm in the North Fairlington Community Center, 3001 South Abingdon Street. The tour, now held every three years, benefits the Fairlington Historical Society (FHS). If you can’t attend but have questions, please email us at homeandgarden@fairlingtonhistoricalsociety.org
